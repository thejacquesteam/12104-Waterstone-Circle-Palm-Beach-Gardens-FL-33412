Overview

Polished design, effortless flow, and a lifestyle that feels distinctly South Florida.

Set in La Terre at Avenir, this newer-construction ranch-style residence pairs open-concept living with refined finishes and a calm, modern atmosphere.

Built in 2022, this single-story home at 12104 Waterstone Circle offers 2,852 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space with a clean, elevated feel throughout. The open layout connects the great room, chef’s kitchen, and private outdoor areas in a way that makes everyday living and entertaining feel easy and intentional. Details like the coffered ceiling, quartz countertops, oversized island, upgraded cabinetry to the ceiling, and custom California Closets pantry bring a tailored finish to the interior. The primary suite adds a spa-style bath, soaking tub, dual vanities, and a custom closet system, creating a quiet retreat inside a modern, low-maintenance home. With Avenir’s newer community setting and Palm Beach Gardens convenience, the property offers a balanced mix of style, comfort, and access.
